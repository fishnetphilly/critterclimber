House Wren — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

About House Wren

House Wren is a personal desktop application used to organize and evaluate housing listings in Philadelphia.

Google Account Access

With the account owner's authorization, House Wren uses Google's Gmail API with read-only permission to retrieve housing alert emails from a dedicated Gmail account.

House Wren does not send, delete, or modify Gmail messages.

Data Storage and Use

Housing listing information is processed and stored locally on the user's computer. Google authorization credentials are stored locally using Windows data protection.

House Wren does not sell Gmail data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties. Gmail data is used only to support the application's housing-listing functionality.

Data Retention and Deletion

Imported listing records remain on the user's computer until removed by the user. Google access can be revoked through the application and through Google Account permissions.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact housewren38@gmail.com.
